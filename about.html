About KeyTonic

KeyTonic is an independent educational project focused on everyday computer keyboard use. It is for people who want to solve a frustrating key issue, make their desktop workflow easier, or learn a handful of reliable shortcuts without browsing a hundred conflicting lists.

What we publish

We write task-specific guides for Windows and Mac keyboard conventions, everyday editing, application focus, connectivity problems, compact layout decisions, and keyboard accessibility. Our interactive tools are lightweight reference and practice aids; they do not inspect your computer, diagnose hardware, or upload your typing.

Who is responsible?

KeyTonic is presented as a small independent editorial website, not as a manufacturer service center or an official partner of Apple, Microsoft, Google, or Mozilla. We do not claim professional hardware-repair credentials. Readers should consult manufacturer documentation for repair work, battery safety, and current version-specific instructions.
