Privacy policy

Effective: 11 November 2026. KeyTonic is a static educational website. The Shortcut Finder, Key Issue Pathfinder and Typing Studio run using JavaScript in your browser. They do not upload entered text, shortcut searches, device symptoms or practice passages to a KeyTonic server, and they do not create a website account.

Advertising and external requests

Editorial pages include the Google AdSense script for verification and potential ad serving. Google and its partners may use cookies or similar identifiers when ads are enabled, subject to applicable law, consent settings and Google policies. We cannot promise that no third-party data processing occurs. See Google advertising technologies and our advertising disclosure.

Fonts and outbound links

Our design requests typefaces from Google Fonts; that provider may receive connection metadata such as IP address and user agent. Clicking external documentation links sends you to the destination website under its own privacy practices. Standard hosting and security infrastructure may process connection logs to serve the pages.

Information you send by email

If you email raflipanel2@gmail.com, your email address and message are processed by the email provider and used to respond or review the issue. Do not send sensitive device logs or passwords. To request deletion of a message, contact the same address with enough detail to identify it.

Changes and questions

We may update this notice as tools, hosting or advertising implementations change. Check the effective date above and contact us for questions. This notice describes the current source build; live deployment settings, regional consent requirements and provider configurations should be reviewed by the publisher before launch.
